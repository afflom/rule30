# Rule 30 Problem 1: equivalent forms

Rule 30 Prize Problem 1 asks whether the center column of rule 30, started from a single black cell, is ever eventually periodic. It is open. This folder collects statements that are **proved equivalent** to it in Lean 4 + Mathlib. A proof of any one of them is a proof of Problem 1.

```lean
-- the center column read as the binary number 0.c(0)c(1)c(2)…
noncomputable def centerBinary : ℝ := ∑' t, (if c t then 1 else 0) / 2 ^ (t + 1)

theorem problem1_iff_irrational_binary : Rule30Prize.Problem1 ↔ Irrational centerBinary
```

**Status:**
- 52 of the 54 equivalent forms in [CATALOG.md](CATALOG.md) are proved here, as 163 theorems named `problem1_iff_*`.
- For [46] with integer bases, for the frozen-boundary conjecture, for Prize Problem 3 and for the stronger statements of catalog Section 4, the direction "the statement implies Problem 1" is proved: `problem1_of_*`.
- Prize Problem 2 (density 1/2) is proved equivalent to its limit form, to its Abel-mean form and to the simple normality of x₂ in base 2 (`problem2_iff_tendsto`, `problem2_iff_abel`, `problem2_iff_simplyNormal`), and to the same density question for rules 86, 135 and 149 (`Symmetry.problem2_iff_rule86`, `problem2_iff_rule135`, `problem2_iff_rule149`); the Abel-mean form needs the Hardy–Littlewood–Karamata Tauberian theorem, proved in Tauberian. It is also equivalent to the convergence of the iterated Cesàro (Hölder) means and of the Cesàro means (C, k) of every order (`HolderMeans.problem2_iff_holder`, through Hardy's Tauberian theorem; `CesaroMeans.problem2_iff_cesaroMean`, through Toeplitz's lemma and the Abel mean), to the k-th black cell occurring at time 2k + o(k), or the mean gap between black cells tending to 2 (`BlackTimes.problem2_iff_trueTime`, `problem2_iff_mean_gap`), and to every limit point of the empirical measures of c giving the cylinder [1] mass 1/2 (`EmpiricalMeasures.problem2_iff_mapClusterPt`). Normality gives Problem 2 (`problem2_of_isNormal`).

## Building

This is a Lake project on Lean 4.35.0. It pins Mathlib at commit `56a005855e`, its `v4.35.0` tag, and depends on the core-Lean snapshot in [`../lean`](../lean) for `Rule30Prize.Problem1`.

From the kit root, which shares one Mathlib with this folder:

```sh
lake exe cache get        # prebuilt Mathlib (or sh setup.sh)
lake build                # builds the kit, the catalog and this library; about 1 minute here
lake env lean equivalents/scripts/CheckAxioms.lean   # axioms of all problem1_iff_*, problem1_of_*, problem2_iff_* and problem2_of_* theorems
```

## Adding an equivalent form

1. Pick an entry from [CATALOG.md](CATALOG.md) whose status is *Stated*. Its suggested Lean statement is in the entry.
2. Prove a general theorem for every `b : ℕ → Bool`, then specialize it to `c` as `Rule30Prize.Problem1 ↔ X`.
3. Watch for:
   - double expansions in base 2;
   - eventually constant sequences (`Rule30Prize.center_not_eventually_constant` handles them for c);
   - `0 < P`.
4. Keep the file free of `sorry`, `native_decide` and new axioms. Add it to `Rule30Equivalents.lean`, and regenerate `scripts/CheckAxioms.lean` with `python3 equivalents/scripts/gen_check_axioms.py` from the kit root.
5. Have someone other than the author restate the main theorem from scratch and check that it says what it claims.

The hard classical one left is Durand's theorem ([46]).
